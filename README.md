# OMNARIA

**LATHAMAUDIO flagship synthesizer.**

OMNARIA is being designed as a production-first electronic-music instrument. Its ordinary sounds must compete directly with the best modern synths on tone, workflow and CPU before experimental ideas are allowed to count as advantages.

## Product rule

OMNARIA does not currently claim a new synthesis method. If development produces a mathematically distinct sound-generation engine, it will be named only after the mechanism is defined, tested against prior art and shown to create a genuinely useful class of musical behaviour.

## Non-negotiable release gates

- Core oscillators/unison must be clean, wide, punchy and CPU-conscious.
- Bass, pluck, supersaw, pad, lead and sequence sounds must stand on their own without experimental processing.
- Filters, envelopes, modulation, effects and sampling must meet flagship quality rather than prototype quality.
- The centre globe must visualise real engine/performance state rather than decorative animation.
- MIDI learn, automation, preset management and DAW recall are product requirements.
- No flagship release based on feature-count parity alone.

Development continues on the OMNARIA flagship foundation. Experimental synthesis ideas are being preserved as research until the underlying mathematics and DSP justify stronger claims.
